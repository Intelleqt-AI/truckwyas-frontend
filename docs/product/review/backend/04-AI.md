# 04 · AI/ML capability review — TruckWys backend

Reviewed: `truckwys-backend` main @ `45039ee` (read-only), plus web-app strings in `truckwyas-frontend/src` and marketing copy in `truckways-website/app/ai/page.tsx` and `lib/site.ts`. No live LLM calls were made. Every probe ran with provider keys blanked and the `anthropic.Anthropic` and `openai.OpenAI` constructors monkeypatched to raise. No DB writes. The copilot probe stubs `_capital_summary` so the cache table is never written. `git status --porcelain` is empty.
Scratch artefacts (all in this folder): `extraction_eval.py` + `_output.txt`, `extraction_marketing_example_output.txt`, `winprob_probe.py` + `_output.txt`, `prompt_size_probe.py` + `_output.txt`.
Known issues in audit section 3 (email tamper, paid-invoice demands, reminder idempotency, draft-only boundary, serializer actor context, update snapshot binding, provider auto-selection disabling tools) are not re-reported here. Some findings below build on them. Briefing "outstanding" inflation is also already known (#50). It is only quantified here.

## Verdict

TruckWys has **one real ML system**: the quote win-probability model. It is logistic regression or gradient boosting, learned from `QuoteOutcome`. Its training infrastructure is better than most seed-stage products have: user and global tiers, a feature-schema version, an AUC regression gate, a price-sensitivity gate, atomic artifact saves, and honest "blocker" reporting. It has **never trained on this dev DB** (2 outcomes, 0 `MLModelVersion` rows, empty `media/ml_models`).

It also has **three genuine LLM features**: quote extraction, the copilot (with tools and RAG over invoices), and briefing/narratives. They are reasonably grounded, but have no evals, no cost telemetry and no protection against untrusted text in prompts.

Almost everything else labelled "AI" is **deterministic rules**:
- the customer "AI risk" score
- the "AI health score"
- the 7-pillar underwriting engine (about a quarter of its points are hard-coded assumptions)
- collections (tone templates, not scheduled)
- the "confidence" numbers

The most serious honesty problems:

1. **The website promises the opposite of how the system works.** It says "will not learn from other fleets… never shared or pooled" and "starts only after ~40 of *your* completed loads". In fact the fallback model pools every tenant, and the 40-sample gate is per user or platform-wide, not per fleet.
2. **Heuristic win probabilities still reach users as if they were predictions.** They are gated in `/quotes/analyze/`, but they leak through:
   - `/quotes/win-probability/` and `/quotes/suggest/`
   - the `/analyze/` rationale text
   - the QuoteBuilder save payload, which persists the heuristic percentage and a hard-coded `confidence: "MEDIUM"` on every quote. This is the banned 63%/MEDIUM default in a new form.

**Overall:** the engineering is honest in the places people looked, and dishonest at the edges. Fix the copy and the three leak paths first (S effort). Then fix leakage and calibration before any model goes live (M effort).

## Findings (ranked)

### F1 · HIGH · The website's data-pooling and cold-start promises are false
**Evidence**
- Site copy: `app/ai/page.tsx:193` says "It will not learn from other fleets… Your data is never shared or pooled". Lines 190 and 53 say recommendations start only after "about 40 of **your** completed loads". Line 211 says "starts learning your fleet from the first load".
- **Reality 1, the model pools tenants.** `quote_training.build_win_training_matrix_for_scope('global')` (`quote_training.py:48-79`) trains on every company's outcomes. `WinProbabilityModel.resolve_for_user` (`quote_ml.py:551-590`) falls back to that pooled model for any user with fewer than 40 of their own outcomes. A brand-new fleet therefore gets "Platform AI" prices built from other fleets' data on day one.
- **Reality 2, the gate is not per fleet.** It counts 40 decided *quotes* (accepted plus rejected) per *user* (`created_by`), or 40 platform-wide. It is not 40 completed loads per fleet.
- **Reality 3, cross-tenant data feeds pricing features and underwriting.**
  - `lane_benchmark.resolve_market_rate` tier 1-2 (`lane_benchmark.py:408-426`) uses other operators' won quotes, with k=5 across ≥2 operators. With only two operators, each can infer the other's median.
  - `RiskEngine._get_operator_avg_invoice_amount` uses `Invoice.objects.all()` across every tenant (`risk_engine.py:1215-1218`).

**Impact:** a misleading-marketing exposure (CPA s41) and a POPIA purpose-limitation problem (pooling needs a lawful basis and disclosure). This is also the claim most likely to be checked by a sophisticated fleet buyer or a lender.

**Fix:**
- **S:** change the copy now: "until your account has enough won *and* lost quotes, recommendations come from an anonymised platform model, or from cost-plus pricing if you opt out".
- **M:** make the global tier opt-in per company. Require ≥5 distinct operators for the benchmark (plus noise). Scope `_get_operator_avg_invoice_amount` to the company.

### F2 · HIGH · Heuristic win probabilities still reach users as model predictions (the 63%/MEDIUM regression)
**Evidence** (`winprob_probe_output.txt`; `resolve_prediction_context` returns `available=False`, so every number below is the sigmoid heuristic):

| Endpoint / input | Output |
|---|---|
| `POST /quotes/win-probability/`, R5,000, any lane including Durban→PMB | `win_probability 0.98` |
| `POST /quotes/win-probability/`, R90,000 JHB→CPT interlink | `0.02` |
| `/quotes/win-probability/`, no lane resolved | Uses the hard-coded `market_rate = 43800.0` (JHB-CPT interlink) **for every lane** (`views_ai_quote.py:1517`) |
| `/quotes/suggest/` | `win_probability 0.54` with `confidence 0.7` (a constant, `:234`) and no `available` or `source` flag (`:282-321`) |

Other leak paths:
- `quote_analysis` gates `ai_prediction` correctly, but `suggested_price_rationale` still says "Maximises expected profit at X% margin **with a Y% win probability**" from the heuristic (`quote_analysis.py:548-553`). The LLM narrative is also handed the heuristic `price_optimization` block and told to "justify the suggested price" (`:329-352`).
- **Frontend, `QuoteBuilder.tsx:1002-1004`:** the save payload always sends `confidence: "MEDIUM"`, and `win_probability` comes from the heuristic whenever `aiAwaitingData` is true.
  - `QuoteDetail.tsx:649-667` then shows "Win probability NN% — Estimated chance of winning at current price".
  - `QuotesList.tsx:157` shows a "Medium" confidence badge on every quote.

**Impact:** users price real loads from numbers that are a hand-tuned curve. The 98%/2% saturation is untrustworthy on sight. This directly contradicts the "It will not invent a price… the screen says so" claim.

**Fix (S):**
- Server side: return `win_probability: null` plus `win_probability_source: 'heuristic'|'model_user'|'model_global'` on all three endpoints. Never put the heuristic percentage in rationale or narrative text.
- Frontend: persist `win_probability` only when `ai_prediction.available`. Delete the hard-coded `confidence`, or derive it from model sample size and Brier score.
- Replace the 43,800 fallback with "no market reference" (→ `price_ratio_available=0`).

### F3 · HIGH · Train/serve skew: two serving endpoints would feed a trained model garbage
**Evidence:** `/quotes/win-probability/` (`views_ai_quote.py:1558-1565`) and `/quotes/suggest/` (`:289-296`) build only 5 features. The CORE model expects 10 (`quote_features.CORE_FEATURES`), and `vectorize()` zero-fills the missing ones. Once a model trains, these endpoints silently pass:
- `price_ratio_available=0` ("no market reference"), even with a real ratio
- `quoted_margin_pct=0`
- `user_historical_win_rate=0`
- `distance_km=0`

On top of that:
- `client_tier` is derived from the *total* decided count (`:1542-1547`), but training uses the *accepted* count (`quote_features._tier_from_accepted_count`).
- Cold-start `historical_acceptance_rate` is 0.7 in serving and 0.5 in training.
- The history count is not bounded by time.

`/quotes/analyze/` does this correctly via `compute_features`. No web-frontend caller was found for these two endpoints, but they are public API surface.

**Fix (S):** route both through `quote_features.compute_features(...)`, or delete the endpoints.

### F4 · HIGH · Label leakage in "leakage-safe" features; the CV scheme is optimistic
**Evidence:**
- `customer_signals`, `user_signals` and `lane_historical_acceptance_rate` filter prior quotes by `Quote.created_at <= as_of` but read their *current* `outcome` (`quote_features.py:154,183,216`). A quote created before `as_of` and decided after it leaks its future label into the earlier row. The module docstring claims the opposite ("strictly from decided quotes… before as_of").
- The outcome snapshot is computed at decision time with `as_of=quote.created_at` (`quote_outcome_capture.py:160-170`), so it inherits the same leak.
- CV is shuffled `StratifiedKFold` (`quote_training.py:223`), not time-ordered. Leakage plus a random split inflates offline AUC, and the AUC regression gate then compares inflated numbers.
- Also check: if operators edit `total_amount` to the negotiated price before marking a quote accepted, `price_ratio` on accepted rows encodes the outcome.

**Fix (M):** filter by `QuoteOutcome.created_at <= as_of` (the decision time), not by quote creation time. Use a time-ordered holdout (last 20% by `created_at`). Freeze the priced amount at send time (a `sent_total` field).

### F5 · HIGH · Probabilities are uncalibrated but used as probabilities
**Evidence:** `LogisticRegression(class_weight='balanced')` (`quote_training.py:125`) re-weights the classes, which shifts predicted probabilities toward 50% and away from the true base rate. GBM candidates are not calibrated at all. Brier is computed but never gated; only AUC and price sensitivity gate promotion. The optimiser multiplies these probabilities by margin to get "expected profit", so miscalibration moves the recommended price directly.

**Fix (M):**
- Drop `balanced`, or calibrate: Platt or isotonic via `CalibratedClassifierCV` on a time holdout.
- Gate promotion on Brier score and reliability-curve error (ECE ≤ 0.05).
- Show a band ("40–55%") when n < 200.
- Store the reliability curve in `MLModelVersion.evaluation_metrics`.

### F6 · HIGH · Underwriting score is about 25% hard-coded assumptions and prices real advances
**Evidence** (`risk_engine.py`). The table lists the constant contributions, weighted into the 0-100 total:

| Pillar | Constant items | Weighted points |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Business type 7, insurance 5 "(assumed)", industry 2 | 2.1 |
| P2 | Margin trend 12, tax "SARS good standing (assumed)" 10, volatility fixed at `0.20` → 10 (`:1126`) | 6.4 |
| P3 | Customer size 5, payment method 4. Plus 15 more when there is no bureau data | 1.8 (+3.0) |
| P4 | Currency 10, invoice type 5 | 2.25 |
| P6 | Route risk 18, cargo 20, completion "90% (assumed)" 15, driver "clean (assumed)" 8 | 6.1 |
| P7 | The whole pillar is constant, 59 points, including the fabricated fact **"1% default rate (healthy)"** (`:886`), "Stable fuel prices" and "Moderate load shedding" | 5.9 |

That is ≈24.6 fixed points, or ≈27.6 with no bureau, against tier cut-offs at 40/55/70/85. Other structural problems:
- P1's reachable maximum is 55 of 100 by construction.
- A customer with no payment history defaults to `avg_days_to_pay=30`, which scores "excellent" (+25 raw, 5 weighted; `:1185-1213`).
- `confidence_level` starts at a base of 60.
- The ML blend is off (`RISK_ML_WEIGHT=0`). There is nothing to calibrate against (1 `PaymentOutcome`).
- `explain_risk_score` asks an LLM to explain these constants to a lender as real drivers (`llm_insights.py:216-259`, Anthropic-only, no timeout).

**Impact:** fee and advance-% tiers are partly set by constants. This is a credit-decision fairness and explainability problem (POPIA s71 automated decisions; NCA if extended to credit). Lender due diligence will find it.

**Fix (M):**
- Drop the unobserved sub-factors and renormalise weights over observed ones.
- Show "not assessed" instead of assumed facts.
- Route cold-start and insufficient data to manual review, not "excellent".
- Remove the "1% default rate" string.
- Stop calling it institutional-grade until it is backtested on repayment outcomes.

### F7 · MEDIUM-HIGH · Rules and fake statistics labelled "AI" or "confidence"
**Evidence:**
- **Customer risk.** "AI risk" / "AI risk profile" (`Capital.tsx:217`, `CustomerRisk.tsx:105-155`, `CustomerDetail.tsx:196`) is a fixed formula. The deterministic fallback text literally says "has an **AI risk score** of X%" (`customer_risk.py:199`). The risk % is also used as a proportional funding haircut (`fundable_amount`), which treats an uncalibrated score as a loss rate.
- **Vehicles.** "AI health score" is a weighted average (`tasks.py:302`).
- **Risk-ML pipeline.** It says "Training XGBoost model…" but trains sklearn GBM (`train_ml_model.py:41`, `ml_pipeline.py:61-64`). It invents `expected_days_late = proba*60` and `confidence = |p−0.5|·2` (`ml_pipeline.py:290-293`).
- **`/suggest/`.** Constant `confidence 0.7`, or the LLM's self-rated confidence clamped to 0.3–0.95. It returns `source:'openai'` even when Anthropic generated the answer (`views_ai_quote.py:234,273-275`).
- **Benchmark.** `confidence:'high'` at 5 quotes (`:1404,1416`).
- **Website claims not implemented:** risk from "short payments, disputes", and "credit limits sized per client" (no code sets `credit_limit` from a score).

**Fix (S):**
- Rename to "Payment risk (rules)" and "Vehicle health score", and show the formula.
- Remove self-reported LLM confidence.
- Rename "confidence" to "data coverage" wherever it measures completeness.
- Fix or remove the unimplemented claims.

### F8 · MEDIUM · The regex fallback silently corrupts SA-format numbers and routes
**Evidence** (offline eval below). 2 of 20 descriptions were fully correct. Silent numeric errors that flow straight into pricing:
- `"12,5 ton"` → **5,000 kg** (the SA decimal comma is ignored)
- `"22 000 kg"` and `"34 000kg"` → **0 kg** (the SA space thousands separator)
- `"…from Midrand to Centurion tomorrow"`, preceded by "to send", → delivery **"send"**

The website's own example, "20 tons of steel, JHB to Cape Town, flatbed, Tuesday", loses the pickup (and the date) on this path. "JHB-CPT", "Germiston to Polokwane" and "Witbank to Richards Bay" (routes without "from") all lose the pickup. The regex response has **no `source` field**, so the UI cannot tell users they are on the degraded path (the LLM path returns `source:'llm'`). Weight has no plausibility bound.

**Fix:**
- **S:** normalise `(\d{1,3})[  ](\d{3})` and `(\d+),(\d+)` before matching. Add `X-Y`, `X to Y` and `X - Y` route patterns with a gazetteer (Germiston, Witbank/eMalahleni, Gqeberha, Polokwane, City Deep, Richards Bay…). Return `source:'rules'` and `field_provenance`. Reject weights over 60 t for a single vehicle.
- **M:** move the regex out of the view into a testable `quote_extract_rules.py` with this eval set as a regression test.

### F9 · MEDIUM · Untrusted text reaches the copilot prompt with no data framing (builds on the known email/update issues)
**Evidence:**
- **System prompt.** It embeds the snapshot JSON: quote `cargo` free text, customer names and companies, and billing-audit rows (`agent.py:741-767`).
- **RAG block.** It embeds invoice `Notes:` text (`rag.py:88-89` → `agent.py:389-409`) under a heading that frames it as trustworthy records.
- **Tool results.** These are appended as `role:tool` messages verbatim (`agent.py:626`). Only `USER MEMORY` is framed as "DATA, never instructions" (`agent.py:563-572`). `prompt_size_probe_output.txt` confirms that framing is absent from the snapshot and RAG sections.

Attack path: invoice notes, cargo and expense text can be written by any tenant user with create rights, or imported from Xero/QuickBooks/CSV. That text can then steer an **admin's** copilot into drafting a `propose_send_email` or `propose_delete`. Human confirmation is the only barrier, and audit items #23/#26/#28 show confirmation does not bind content today.

**Fix (M):**
- Wrap snapshot, RAG and tool output in `<untrusted_data>` with an explicit rule.
- Tag proposals whose arguments contain retrieved text ("influenced by record X").
- Block `propose_*` in any turn where the user's message did not request a write.
- Add 15–20 injection cases to an eval suite.

### F10 · MEDIUM · POPIA data minimisation in the copilot and RAG
**Evidence:**
- **Drivers.** Every copilot turn sends *all* drivers' email, licence number, violations and **accident history** (`agent.py:222-240`).
- **Customers.** It sends up to 100 customer contacts (email, phone, credit limit), whatever the question. On the demo tenant that is about 5.4k tokens of system prompt per turn (20 contacts, 16 drivers). The tool loop resends it up to 6 times (`agent.py:591`).
- **RAG.** Embedding sends every invoice document, notes included, to OpenAI (`rag.py:94-109`).

What is already good: banking details are minimised (`agent.py:733-739`), and `privacy/page.tsx` discloses Anthropic/OpenAI and s72 transfers.

Missing:
- a per-tenant AI off-switch
- any evidence in the repo of a DPA or zero-data-retention setting
- retention for `CopilotMessage` (kept indefinitely)
- company scoping on `CopilotConversation` (memory is scoped, conversations are not)

**Fix (M):**
- Drop `drivers` and `contacts` from the snapshot and serve them via `query_records` on demand.
- Never send licence or accident fields unless the question is about that driver.
- Add `Company.ai_processing_enabled`.
- Add a 12-month copilot-message retention.
- Record the provider DPA and ZDR status in the privacy page.

### F11 · MEDIUM · No model ops: evals, cost, timeouts, versioning
**Evidence:**
- **No cost telemetry.** Zero token or usage logging anywhere (grep `usage.`/`input_tokens` finds nothing).
- **No LLM evals.** The 141 AI-related tests mock the LLM. `test_title_generated_from_first_message` fails whenever no key is set.
- **Expensive model defaults.** Agent, briefing and extraction all default to `claude-opus-4-8` (`agent.py:32`, `llm_insights.py:25`, `llm_quote.py:36`).
- **Missing timeouts.** Clients built without a timeout in `llm_quote.py:453,465` and `llm_insights.py:243` take the SDK default (600 s). `agent.py` sets 30 s.
- **Briefing endpoint.** It has no copilot throttle scope, is uncached, and re-embeds RAG synchronously on every GET (`llm_insights.py:163-167`).
- **Inconsistent provider selection.** Extraction ignores `COPILOT_LLM_PROVIDER` (`llm_quote.py:345`), and risk explanation is Anthropic-only.
- **No versioning or trace.** Prompts and tool calls are not persisted beyond the reply text.
- **Win model OK.** It does have versioning (`MLModelVersion`).

**Fix (M):** add one `core/services/llm_gateway.py` that owns:
- provider and model selection, timeouts and retries
- token and cost logging per company/feature
- prompt version IDs
- redaction and ZDR headers
- a daily per-tenant budget with graceful degradation to rules

Cache the briefing per company per period (15 min).

### F12 · MEDIUM · Bureau adapter is a stub presented as ready
**Evidence:**
- `bureau_adapter.py` claims "going live is just supplying a credential". The endpoints (for example `api.experian.co.za/business/v1/commercial-score`) and payloads are unverified guesses.

**Fix (M):**

### F13 · MEDIUM · The collections claim is not backed by a schedule
**Evidence:**

**Fix (S):** fix the copy, or add a beat entry, but only after audit items #24/#25/#29 land.

## Extraction eval (regex/fallback path only)

**Setup:** 20 realistic SA load descriptions run through `AIChatQuoteView` via `APIRequestFactory`, as `admin@truckwys.co.za` (tenant "TruckWys (Pty) Ltd"; available types: Flatbed Truck, Rigid Truck, Semi-Trailer Truck). LLM forced off and network constructors blocked; reads only.

**Scoring rules:**
- Location matches on the canonical city or alias (JHB = Johannesburg).
- Vehicle counts as correct if it is either matched to a fleet type or surfaced as an unmatched keyword for create-on-the-fly. Where the input names no vehicle, correct means none was set.
- Trip-type accuracy is inflated, because most inputs have no trip type and absence scores as correct.

| Field | Correct | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| pickup | 11/20 | **55%** |
| delivery | 16/20 | 80% |
| weight | 18/20 | 90% (both misses are *silent wrong values*: 12,5 t → 5,000 kg; "22 000 kg" → 0) |
| cargo | 11/20 | **55%** (captures "coal Witbank", "fertiliser round trip", "furniture Sandton") |
| vehicle | 15/20 | 75% (misses: tri-axle, taut liner, superlink, flat deck, bakkie) |
| trip_type | 20/20 | 100% (mostly trivially absent) |
| **All fields correct** | **2/20** | **10%** |

Extra checks:
- The website's own example, "20 tons of steel, JHB to Cape Town, flatbed, Tuesday": **pickup lost**, date not extracted.
- "Quote 34 000kg maize Bothaville to Durban": pickup, weight (0) and cargo all wrong.

**SA-specific gaps:** SA number formats (`12,5`, `22 000`), routes without "from" (`X to Y`, `JHB-CPT`), SA body names (tri-axle, taut/tautliner, superlink, flat deck, bakkie, side-tipper) and non-gazetteer towns.

**LLM path:** not measured, because no live calls were allowed. Its prompt is strong: abbreviation normalisation, preserving street addresses, relative dates against today, a capacity-aware vehicle match, and JSON-schema output. But it has no eval and no per-field provenance. The same 20 cases should be the first LLM regression set, scored by the scripted scorer in `extraction_eval.py`.

## Target AI architecture

1. **One LLM gateway** (`llm_gateway.py`). It owns:
   - provider and model per feature (Haiku/mini tier for extraction and titles; larger only for copilot reasoning)
   - timeouts ≤ 30 s, retries = 1
   - a prompt registry with version IDs
   - per-call logging to `LLMCallLog(company, feature, model, prompt_version, in_tokens, out_tokens, cost_zar, latency, outcome)`
   - per-tenant daily budget and kill switch (`Company.ai_processing_enabled`)
   - redaction of licence/ID/bank fields
   - ZDR headers where available
2. **Untrusted-data discipline.** Everything that is not the system prompt or the current user message is wrapped as `<untrusted_data source=...>`. Tools return structured JSON with `_untrusted` markers. Write or email proposals are allowed only when the current user turn asks for one. Proposals carry signed snapshots (known packets 035/038).
3. **Grounding checks.** For briefing, narratives and copilot money answers, a post-generation numeric verifier checks every `R` amount and % against the source JSON, or against tool results for the copilot. On failure, fall back to rules or mark the answer "unverified".
4. **Honest UX contract.** Every AI-ish number carries `source ∈ {model_user, model_global, llm, rules, heuristic, estimate}` and `basis` (n samples, operators, date). UI labels:
   - "AI" only for `model_*` and `llm`
   - "Estimate" for heuristic
   - "Rules" for formulas

   Nothing heuristic is persisted into a field later shown as a prediction.
5. **Cold-start rules, enforced server-side and matching the website word for word.**
   - Per-**company** tier: n ≥ 40 decided outcomes with ≥ 10 of each class, and a passing calibration gate.
   - Global tier: opt-in only, ≥ 5 contributing operators.
   - Otherwise: cost-plus target margin and **no probability shown**.
6. **Win-model training hygiene.**
   - decision-time `as_of`
   - time-ordered holdout
   - no class re-weighting, or post-hoc calibration
   - gates: AUC ≥ previous − 0.02, **Brier/ECE**, price sensitivity (existing), minimum class counts
   - a monthly reliability report per tenant
   - shadow mode for two weeks before a new artifact goes live
7. **Underwriting.**
   - rules pre-screen over observed factors only
   - "insufficient data → manual review"
   - bureau via real specs with a consent log
   - a later ML model trained on `PaymentOutcome` with a temporal split, calibration, SHAP explanations, and a fairness check (no proxies for location or race via area)
   - a human-in-the-loop override record for every automated decline (POPIA s71)
8. **Evals as code.** `core/evals/` holds:
   - extraction golden set: these 20 cases, growing to 200 from anonymised real messages
   - copilot Q&A golden set with numeric answers checked against fixtures
   - injection suite
   - briefing numeric-grounding suite

   All of it runs in CI against rules and recorded fixtures, and nightly against live providers with a budget cap.
9. **POPIA.**
   - DPA and ZDR with providers, documented
   - tenant opt-out
   - minimisation (no drivers or contacts in the default snapshot)
   - copilot retention of 12 months
   - pooled-model consent recorded
   - bureau purpose logging
   - PAIA/privacy copy listing every AI feature and the data each one sends
