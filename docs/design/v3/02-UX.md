# 02 · UX craft critique: every TruckWys page

Principal UX review, read-only, 28 Sep 2026, branch `truckwys/design-v3` at http://localhost:3815. The bar is Linear or Stripe: the page's purpose is clear in 5 seconds, the most important thing is the most prominent, text is short, states are honest, and it works by keyboard and on a phone.

This covers UX craft only. The product findings in `docs/product/review/00-DECISIONS.md` (the four jobs, the new navigation, three different "owed" numbers, 20-row lists, fake AI and confidence labels, Fast Pay not being live) are not repeated here. They are referenced only where they create a craft problem on a specific screen.

## How this was done

- **Coverage.** All 56 routes in `src/App.tsx` at 1440 in light and dark (52 app routes plus 4 public ones). 15 core routes at 390 in light and 6 in dark. Three journeys walked end to end. Keyboard pass on one form (`/finance/invoices/new`), one table (`/fleet/vehicles`), one drawer (Add vehicle) and the quote builder. Loading, empty and error states forced on 7 pages.
- **Read-only guarantee.** Every non-GET request was aborted in the browser, so nothing reached the backend. That produces the `BLOCKED POST` lines in the logs. For the quote builder's priced state, `route/calculate`, `quotes/analyze` and `quotes/guard` were answered inside the browser with synthetic payloads, so the figures in `j1-08`/`j1-09` illustrate the layout and are not real prices. No kanban card was dragged and no send, reminder or save button was pressed.
- **The build changed during the review.** The shell, Today (was Overview), Insights and the vehicle and driver detail pages were being rebuilt while this ran. Twice the dev server served a broken build: once `shell.css` was missing, and once `Overview.tsx` had no default export and the app went blank.
  - `light-1440_*` (first pass): mostly the **old** shell with the icon rail.
  - `dark-1440_*`, `*-390_*`, `j*`, `state-*`, `kb-*`: the **new** shell with the labelled grouped sidebar and phone tab bar.
  - Findings on the rebuilt surfaces are marked **[mid-rebuild]**. Treat them as input to that work, not as verdicts.
- **Screenshots** are in `v3/critique/shots-ux/` and paths below are relative to that folder. A name ending `-end` or `-mid` is the same page scrolled inside `main`. Metrics are in `metrics-{theme}-{width}.json` and the scripts are `crawl.mjs`, `j1*.mjs`, `j2.mjs`, `j3.mjs`, `states*.mjs` and `kb.mjs`.

Severity:
- **S1:** blocks or misleads a core task, or is broken.
- **S2:** real friction or inconsistency a paying user will notice.
- **S3:** polish.

## 1. Verdict in six lines

1. **The biggest problems are behaviour, not styling.** Failed requests spin forever or show "No quotes", so the error states lie. The quote builder shows two prices and does not make clear which one gets sent. Customers who are late cannot be chased from the invoice list. Settings and the quote builder are unusable at 390.
2. **It reads "babyish" for three reasons:**
   - Every panel is a boxed card with a question for a title ("Where is this invoice at?", "How is the total made up?", "What does it cost to run?").
   - Every number carries a sentence that repeats it ("Vehicles with status In use.").
   - Methodology is written into the page body instead of behind an info icon.
3. **It reads "wordy" because pages apologise for their own limits.** Examples: "This page holds the 20 most recent of 50 expenses. Search, filters, totals and charts on this page use these 20…" and "Xero isn't configured on this server yet. Add your Xero app's client ID and secret to the backend environment…". These are fixes to make, not copy to show.
4. **The same pattern is solved 3 or 4 ways:** four toast systems, two confirm styles, three loading styles, three row-action styles, two customer lists that disagree, and two fleet dashboards.
5. **Keyboard access fails in basic ways:**
   - No skip link, so there are 17 tab stops before the page content.
   - Table rows cannot be focused, so vehicle and invoice details cannot be reached by keyboard.
   - The location autocomplete cannot be operated by keyboard.
   - Drawers do not move or trap focus.
6. **The new shell and phone tab bar are a clear step up** (labelled groups and 5 tabs plus More). The pages inside them now have to catch up. The wider sidebar is already causing horizontal overflow on Expenses and Admin › Platform health at 1440.

**Target flow.**
- **Fix the filter.** "Overdue" should mean past its due date with an unpaid balance.
- **Chase from the list.** Offer "Send reminder" on every row with a balance past due, plus a bulk "Remind selected".

- **Detail page primary action.** When the invoice is past due, make "Send reminder" the primary. Otherwise make it "Record payment".

## 4. Top 20 UX fixes, ranked

The ranking weighs impact on the three journeys and trust, then reach, then effort.

1. **Make error states honest on every list and detail page.** After React Query's retries give up, replace the spinner or "Loading…" with an inline block reading "Couldn't load invoices. [Retry]", and keep any KPIs that did load. Never render an empty board or "No quotes" when the request failed. Affected: invoices, vehicles, customers, quotes (`state-*-error.png`). Build a shared `<QueryState>` component with loading skeleton, error and empty.
2. **Fix "Overdue" everywhere.** Overdue means `balance > 0 && due_date < today`, whatever the status. Apply it to the chip filter, the row menu, the detail `canRemind` check (add `PARTIALLY_PAID`) and the Insights "never chased" action.
3. **Put a confirm-and-preview sheet on every outbound message** (send invoice, send reminder, send quote, bulk reminders). Show the recipient email, subject, a preview snippet, the reminder count and last-sent date, and a Send button. After sending, show a toast with "View" that links to the activity row.
4. **One price in the quote builder.** Merge "Quote total" and "Suggested price" into one editable Price field. Show the recommended value as a ghost chip beside it ("Recommended R 13 949 · Apply"), with the cost floor and margin next to it. Make the Send button say the amount: "Send R 13 949 to AVI Limited".
5. **Make the quote builder a two-column layout with a sticky price panel.**
   - Left column: load, route and a smaller map (240 px).
   - Right column: the sticky price panel, with Notes above Send and Save.
   - Demote "Clear & new quote" to a ghost button in a "…" menu.
   - Make it a 3-step stepper at under 768 px.
6. **Settings on a phone: turn the side-by-side sub-nav into list → detail.** On desktop, replace the app sidebar with the settings sidebar instead of stacking two.
7. **Add a period switcher to Reports** (This month by default; Last month; Quarter; YTD; Custom) and share its state with Today's KPIs. Lead the P&L with "September profit R x (m%) · vs August ±". Say "No activity" on empty months in the chart.
8. **Make table rows real links.** Each row should contain an `<a href>` on its primary cell (the whole row clickable is fine, but the link must exist) so rows can be focused, opened with Enter or middle-clicked. Apply this to invoices, vehicles, customers, orders, quotes (list view) and expenses.
9. **Fix keyboard basics:**
   - Add a "Skip to content" link.
   - Give the location autocomplete a proper combobox (`role=combobox`, `aria-activedescendant`, Arrow keys, Enter and Esc).
   - Move focus into drawers and dialogs, trap it, and return it to the trigger.
   - Add visible focus rings to the Radix select triggers and the date text input.
10. **Replace one-click state changes with explicit, confirmed actions.** This covers the vehicle and driver status segmented controls, the quote and order status `<select>`s, the user role `<select>` and the security toggles. Use a primary verb button ("Mark in maintenance") with a small confirm for anything that affects money, access or customers, followed by a toast with Undo.
11. **Kill the disclaimer sentences by fixing the cause.** Paginate or load all rows server-side, then delete:
    - "This list holds the 20 most recent of 34…" (Invoices)
    - "This page holds the 20 most recent of 50 expenses…" (Expenses)
    - "…in the loaded list" (Expenses)
    - "Showing 20 of 23 customers" when there is no next page (Customers)

    This is the single biggest cut in words.
12. **Move methodology behind info icons.** This is principle 9 applied everywhere: the Reports chart basis, the Expenses chart basis, the Heatmap basis, the Risk page scoring rule, all 6 Fast Pay paragraphs, and the Company settings helpers. The shared `InfoTip` component exists but is used on only 3 pages.
13. **Retitle question-style panels with 2 to 4 word noun titles.**

    | Current title | New title |
    |---|---|
    | Where is this invoice at? | Activity |
    | How is the total made up? | Breakdown |
    | What does it cost to run? | Running costs |
    | When is it due? | Service |
    | Who is it for, and what is it for? | Details |
    | Did money in cover costs each month? | Revenue vs costs |
    | How late does AVI Limited pay? | Payment history |

    This alone removes most of the "babyish" read.
14. **Remove restating subtitles under KPI tiles.** "Vehicles with status In use.", "Drivers with status Active.", "Planned route distance.", "Ready to take a load." A subtitle is either the period basis or a delta, as principles §5 says.
15. **One customer list.** Delete Settings › Customers (and check Settings › Vehicles against `/fleet/vehicles`), or make them redirects, since Settings › Customers diverges from `/customers`, including the payment terms mismatch (Net 45 vs 30 days). Do the same for `/fleet/overview` and the orphan `/copilot`.
16. **Collapse the Security sessions list** to "This device", then "N other sessions · Sign out all others", with the list behind a disclosure and capped at 10 with "Show all".
17. **Phone-first order on Today, Insights and the list pages.** At 390, put the actionable list (Needs you, Findings, table rows) before KPI tiles. Collapse KPIs into one horizontally scrolling strip or a single summary line.
18. **Tables at 390 become cards everywhere.** Invoices already do this; apply it to orders, vehicles, customers and expenses. Cap header actions at one primary action plus a "…" menu.
19. **Unify feedback primitives:**
    - One toast system (`@/lib/toast`). Remove the direct `sonner` imports in FleetImport and Xero, `react-toastify` in NotificationSettings, and the page-local `fin-toast` and `flash`.
    - One confirm dialog (the `ConfirmDialog` pattern). Replace the 6 native `confirm()` calls.
20. **One formatting layer for money, numbers and dates.**
    - Money is always `R 20 505,65` in tables and `R 499 530` in KPIs.
    - Dates are always `5 Jun 2026` (never `2026-06-05`, `Sept` or `2022-07-31`).
    - Rates use `R 10,00/km`.
    - Remove the 18 hand-built `` `R${…}` `` templates, which produce "~R2279", "R4,499", "R10.00" and "R23.50".
